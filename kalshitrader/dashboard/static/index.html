Guide

  

    
What the bot does
Every scan it reads the markets you switched on under Markets. When a price dips far enough below its recent high, it buys, and sells when the price bounces back to the target. If a stop is set and the price keeps falling, it sells there instead. Optional research (tennis only, needs an Anthropic key) adds a fair-value estimate; without it the bot trades the price action alone.

    
Status pill
Running = the bot may open positions. Paused = it only manages exits. PAPER/LIVE shows whether orders are simulated or real.

    
Equity
Cash plus the current value of open positions. The small number beside it is today's realised profit or loss.

    
Pause / Resume
Pause stops new buys immediately. Sell targets and stops on open positions keep working.

    
Open positions
Bought is the price paid per contract (cents; a contract pays 100¢ if that side wins). Sells at is the take-profit target. Stop is where the bot sells to cap a loss. Now is the current bid, and P&L is what closing now would make.

    
Markets being watched
LIVE means contracts actually traded recently, and the number beside it is how many. That is the bot's test for a market being in play, because scheduled start times slip constantly. P(win), Form and Comeback come from the optional tennis research and stay blank elsewhere. Decision is the bot's latest call for that side: BUY, or PASS with the reason (not live yet, no dip yet, edge too thin…).

    
Settings ⚙
Toggles and numbers that control the bot, grouped as Bot (on/off switches), Entry (when to buy), Exit (when to sell) and Risk (how much). Each has a ? with an explanation. Saving applies on the next scan.

    
API keys
Your Kalshi key ID and private key let the bot read your balance and, in live mode, place orders. The Anthropic key powers the optional tennis research and screenshot reading. Keys are stored only in the local .env file.

    
Analyze a screenshot
Upload a Kalshi screenshot instead of using the API. You get the same research and a verdict per player.

    
Full dashboard
